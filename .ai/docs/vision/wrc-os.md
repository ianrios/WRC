# WHY? Record Company Operating System (WRC-OS)

## Purpose

WRC-OS is the internal coordination layer for WHY? Record Company.
It serves both human leadership (CEO) and autonomous agents (Engineering, Marketing).

Instagram and other platforms are output surfaces.
WRC-OS is the primary operational interface.

## Departments

- CEO (Vision, Strategy, Overrides)
- Engineering (Build infrastructure and tooling)
- Marketing (Content and campaign execution)

Engineering must exist before Marketing is activated.

## Guiding Principles

1. Infrastructure before automation
2. Human override at every layer
3. Deterministic systems before probabilistic systems
4. All work is represented as structured state
5. All agents operate within scoped authority

## Long Term Vision

A calm, unified dashboard where:

- Media is uploaded
- Campaigns are defined
- Posts are scheduled
- Agents execute
- Instagram is never opened
